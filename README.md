**Gogglebrian’s Race Overhaul**: a vanilla-flavored overhaul of Skyrim’s races, with the goal of ensuring that every race has a particular niche, playstyle, or roleplaying aesthetic in which to thrive.

**Features**

* Improved or expanded racial passive Abilities (or Lesser Powers) for most races  
* Revised starting skill bonuses for Imperials and Argonians (vanilla for all other races)  
* Two versions with different approaches to racial once-a-day Powers:  
  * **Powers version**: rebalances some of the vanilla racial Powers, and adds new Powers to Khajiit and Wood Elf  
  * **Passives Only version**: removes racial Powers entirely and replaces each one with an additional player-only passive ability  
* Balanced? Probably not. Depending on what race you choose and what mods you use, you may want to crank up the difficulty a notch.

**Breakdown by race – from most radical changes to least**

**Khajiit**

* **Preternatural Reflexes Power:** Slow time and avoid all physical damage, 30s  
* **Preternatural Reflexes Passive:** \+20% movement speed  
* **Superior Senses Lesser Power:** Detect nearby actors (freely usable lesser power)  
* **Night-eye:** Vanilla night-eye (freely usable lesser power)  
* **Khajiit Claws:** 20% sneak attack damage (all weapons including unarmed)  
* **Khajiit Claws:** 22 base unarmed damage  
* **Khajiit Agility:** Immune to fall damage, and jump 50% higher (requires [powerofthree’s Tweaks](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/51073))

**Wood Elf**

* **One with Nature Power**: 3x health/magicka/stamina regen, 60s *(actually \+1.4/+6/+10 to the respective base rates)*  
* **One with Nature Passive:** Mix 50% stronger potions with beneficial or restorative effects  
* **Command Animal:** Nearby animals will fight for you, 10m (freely usable lesser power)  
  * An optional esp is included which overhauls Command Animal further, changing it into a 0-cost spell which allows you to make one animal at a time into a temporary follower. I’m keeping it separate for now as it’s script-based and experimental.  
* **Bosmer Acuity:** \+35% ranged sneak attack damage  
* **Resist Disease:** 50% resist disease  *(note: vanilla poison resistance removed)*

**Imperial**

* **Voice of the Emperor Power**: Calm nearby humans, 60s (vanilla)  
* **Voice of the Emperor Passive**: Illusion spells work on people 15 levels higher  
* **Alessian Valor:** Buff nearby allies and bystanders’ health and damage, 2m (freely usable lesser power)  
* **Imperial Fortitude:** \+25% healing spell magnitude   
* **Imperial Fortitude:** \+100 carry weight  
* Skill bonuses: \+10 Speech; \+5 Restoration, Block, Enchanting, Heavy Armor,  and Illusion

**Redguard**

* **Adrenaline Rush Power:** \+20% physical damage and 10x stamina regen, 60s (buffed from vanilla) *(actually \+50 base stamina rate)*  
* **Adrenaline Rush Passive:** 2x stamina regen *(actually \+5 base stamina rate)*  
* **Way of the Sword:** \+15% damage with swords and greatswords and they cut through 50% of armor  
* **Redguard Blood:** \+50 stamina and 50% resist poison  
* **Redguard Mysticism:** 25% stronger Armor spells with 2x duration

**Argonian**

* **Histskin Power**: 10x faster healing, 60s *(vanilla; actually \+10 base heal rate)*  
* **Histskin Passive:** 50% faster health regen *(actually \+0.35 base healing rate)*  
* **Argonian Blood:** 50% resist disease and poison  
* **Argonian Secrets:** Mix 50% stronger poisons, and poisons applied to weapons last for 5x as many hits  
* **Waterbreathing:** Yes. I’ll also mention here that they still have their vanilla 10 unarmed damage.  
* Skill bonuses: \+10 Alchemy; \+5 Lockpick, Alteration, Pickpocket, Restoration, and Sneak

**Orc**

* **Berserker Rage Power:** \+20% physical damage and take half physical damage, 60s (nerfed from vanilla)  
* **Berserker Rage Passive:** \+25% power attack damage  
* **Magic Resistance:** 10% resist magic  
* **Orcish Craftsmanship**: 25% better tempering and enchanting on Orcish equipment

**Dark Elf** 

* **Ancestor’s Wrath Power**: Flame cloak and 50% to avoid physical damage, 60s (buffed from vanilla)  
* **Ancestor Guardian Passive:** 10% chance to avoid physical damage  
* **Dunmer Blood:** \+15% Destruction damage (spells and enchantments)  
* **Dunmer Blood:** 75% resist Fire

**Nord**

* **Battle Cry Power**: Frighten and stagger nearby actors, 30s (vanilla)  
* **Terror of the North Passive:** 25% more likely to stagger targets  
* **Nordic Blood:** 50% resist Frost  
* **Nordic Blood:** 20% reduced shout cooldown  
* **Nordic Blood:** \+50% Frost damage (spells and enchantments)

**High Elf**

* **Highborn Power:** 25x magicka regen, 60s (Vanilla) (*actually \+25 base magicka rate)*  
* **Highborn Passive:** 50% faster magicka regen *(actually \+1.5 base magicka rate)*  
* **Aldmeri Supremacy:** \+50 magicka  
* **Aldmeri Will:** \+5 magnitude and \+50% duration for Illusion spells

**Breton**

* **Dragonskin Power:** 50% spell absorption, 60s (Vanilla)  
* **Dragonskin Passive:** 15% spell absorption  
* **Magic Resistance:** 25% resist magic  
* **Breton Wisdom:** 2x duration for Conjuration spells 

**Soft Requirements**

* [SPID](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/36869) (or [SPID VR](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/59121)) – (Optional) This is necessary for NPCs to properly benefit from several of the new racial passive bonuses. Skip it if you don’t mind that inequity.  
* [powerofthree’s Tweaks](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/51073) – (Optional) Jumping Bonus Fix is required for the Khajiit jump bonus to actually take effect.

**Compatibility**

* Will likely conflict with any mod that changes Races, racial Powers, racial Abilities, or the underlying effects/perks thereof.  
* May conflict with mods changing the **Concentrated Poison** perk, or otherwise using a Set function on the Mod Poison Dose Count perk entry point. (The vanilla perk sets this value to 2; I changed it to multiply the value by 2, so it can subsequently be multiplied elsewhere like by my Argonian Secrets ability.).
